The AI product you use sits on top of a larger economic chain.

Customers pay applications. Applications may pay model providers. Model providers pay for compute, and compute ultimately depends on hardware.

AI Economics · Go deeper

Each layer has a different cost structure and source of advantage.

Infrastructure is capital intensive, frontier models require major research and compute investment, and applications can often start by using models that already exist.

The structural economics

LayerMain pressureWhat can create advantage
