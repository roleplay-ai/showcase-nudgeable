Image & Video

Generative visual models create new images from learned visual patterns and a prompt.

They are not simply searching for an existing photograph. They generate a new visual arrangement that matches the prompt.

Why video is harder

An image must look coherent once. A video has to keep identity, objects, motion and lighting coherent across many frames.

Same character across three frames.

Key insight: image generation creates spatial coherence. Video generation must also preserve coherence over time.
