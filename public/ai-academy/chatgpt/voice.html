Use Voice when speaking improves the work

💭

Think out loud

Explore an idea with less friction and give ChatGPT richer context than a short typed prompt.

🎭

Rehearse

Practice an interview, presentation, difficult conversation or another language.

🚶

Go hands-free

Continue a useful conversation while walking, commuting or away from the keyboard.

Choose conversation or dictation

🗣️

Voice conversation

Best when the back-and-forth itself helps you think, learn or coordinate.

⌨️

Dictation

Best when you only want speech turned into editable text for a message or prompt.

🔎
Listen critically

Voice can make the same factual mistakes as text ChatGPT. Verify important dates, numbers, locations and decisions.

Understand the different Voice experiences

Voice is not one single mode. The available experience and supported capabilities depend on the device, plan, workspace and product version.

🌊

Live

The newest experience supports more natural interruption and back-and-forth conversation, with web search, memory and mixed text or image interaction where those capabilities are available.

📱

Advanced

The earlier real-time experience remains useful on supported mobile devices when video or screen sharing is important and those features are available.

🔁

Standard

Turn-by-turn Voice transcribes what you say before producing a response. It is less fluid than Live but still useful for spoken questions and answers.
