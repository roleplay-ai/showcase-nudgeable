Add the source materialProvide content, data, files, links and rules.
Review the previewCheck copy, links, layout, forms and behavior.

Treat version one as a working prototype

Specific feedback on the visible Site improves the result faster than rewriting one very long prompt.

Give the first Site enough real material

A Site becomes useful when the prompt describes an audience and job, then supplies realistic content, data and rules.

📈

Team reporting

Provide the KPIs, risks, decisions and next actions that the dashboard should surface. Explain which filters or comparisons help the team make decisions.

🎓

Training hub

Supply the learning resources, videos and exercises, then describe how employees should move through them and what completion should look like.

🧪

Workflow prototype

Describe the user, the sequence of actions and the decision you want to test. The prototype should help people react to the workflow, not merely admire the visual design.

Before you publish

🔗

Test the experience

Open links, submit forms, check sign-in behavior and try interactive features as the intended visitor.

👥

Confirm the audience

Site sharing and any sign-in built inside the Site are separate controls. Check both.

🧹

Remove risky material

Do not publish confidential, sensitive or third-party content you do not have the right to share.

Know where Sites stops

🏗️

Good fit

A lightweight app, portal, tracker, dashboard or learning hub where the experience itself is the main outcome.

🧱

Consider a full software build

Use conventional development when the hard part is a specialised backend, private infrastructure or regulated data handling.

Versioning, domains and technical limits

Publishing involves more than pressing a button. Preserve a return point and understand the limits of the hosting environment.

💾

Save a version before deployment

A saved version gives you a clear state to return to if a later change creates a problem. The preview remains the place to review changes before they affect the live Site.

🌍

Custom domains require ownership

Where supported, you can connect a domain you already own by adding the DNS records provided by ChatGPT. Sites does not register or purchase the domain for you.

🏗️

Some architectures will not fit

Specialized frameworks, private networks, databases, background services or strict infrastructure requirements may fall outside the supported hosting runtime. A conventional software build may be more appropriate.
