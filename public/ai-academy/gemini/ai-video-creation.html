Create videos with Gemini tools

Gemini creates or edits a clip through conversation, Flow develops controlled scenes, and Vids assembles a complete workplace video with structure, narration and collaboration.

Access depends on plan, licence, age, region and the selected generation model.

Prompt movement, not only appearance

A medium-wide shot of a product designer in a bright modern studio. She places a sketchbook beside a laptop, the camera slowly pushes in, warm afternoon light moves across the desk, and the screen shows a clean prototype. Natural room tone, no dialogue, 16:9.

A video brief should describe what changes over time, the camera behaviour and any audio requirements.

Move up only when the output needs it

💬

Stop with Gemini

If one conversationally generated clip is the complete deliverable, no larger production workspace is needed.

🎞️

Move to Flow

Use it when you need deliberate continuity, consistent references, editing or several connected clips.

📽️

Finish in Vids

Use it when generated clips are only one part of a narrated workplace video.

💡
Practical rule

Gemini creates the clip, Flow develops the sequence and Vids assembles the complete workplace video.

Ingredients and frames solve different problems

🧩

Ingredients preserve visual references

Reuse people, products, objects or environments across clips. Clean, consistent references are easier to follow.

🖼️

Frames anchor the transition

A start frame defines the beginning; an end frame defines where the clip should finish. The prompt describes the motion between them.

Keep instructions and references aligned

↔️

Avoid conflicting inputs

A prompt that contradicts the reference makes continuity harder to preserve.

🗂️

Keep previous versions

An edit may improve one element while damaging another. Flow history lets you return to a stronger version.

🎬

Use Scenebuilder for sequence

Arrange, trim, reorder and preview supported clips as one visual progression.

Vids adds the business layer

🧱

Structure and script

Create an outline, scene text and narration from a prompt and related Drive material.

🎙️

Voice and recordings

Combine AI voiceover, your own recordings, generated clips and existing media.

👥

Editing and collaboration

Adjust timing, replace media and work with others on the complete video.

Review before publishing

👤

Real people and brands

Check likeness, product appearance, logos and unintended changes.

🔤

Text and continuity

Inspect captions, signs, object consistency, hands and motion across cuts.

©️

Rights and appropriate use

Confirm you have permission to use uploaded images, video and audio.

🛡️
Generated video still needs editorial review

The tools can create convincing motion while introducing incorrect text, inconsistent identity or unintended changes.
