You're probably still using AI in a browser tab

Most people never install the apps they're already paying for, and stay stuck opening a tab every time.

Why it happens

A browser tab is the default first touchpoint, so it becomes the only one, even after the apps exist.

What to do

The mobile and desktop apps let you work with AI while walking, commuting, or away from your desk, and voice mode makes dictating a prompt almost effortless.

Download the app you're already paying for and try voice mode once this week.
