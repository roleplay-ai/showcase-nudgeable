Replace "does this feel right" with a rubric

Judging AI output by gut feel is inconsistent from one day to the next.

Why it happens

"Good" is vague and shifts depending on your mood, your deadline, and how tired you are when you read it.

What to do

Before you review anything important, write down three or four things you're actually checking for. Score against those.

"Score this against: accuracy, completeness, tone match, and clarity."
