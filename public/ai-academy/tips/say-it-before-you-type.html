Say it before you type it

Typing a prompt forces you to compress your thinking before you've finished having it.

Why it happens

Speech is faster and more natural than typing, so it captures the intent before you start editing yourself.

What to do

Talk it through first, then tighten the wording in text. This matters most for anything complicated.

Dictate your first attempt, then edit the transcript before sending it.
