A table is easier to check than a paragraph

When accuracy matters, ask for the answer in a format you can actually audit.

Why it happens

A paragraph hides its own errors inside flowing sentences, where a wrong fact reads just as smoothly as a right one.

What to do

A table with clear columns, like claim, source, and confidence, exposes errors immediately.

"Give me this as a table with columns for claim, source, and confidence."
