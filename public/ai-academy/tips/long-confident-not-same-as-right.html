Long and confident isn't the same as right

A single detailed answer feels finished. It isn't verified.

Why it happens

Length creates an illusion of thoroughness that has nothing to do with accuracy.

What to do

Before you act on anything that matters, ask for the short version first, check if it holds up, then decide whether the long one is worth trusting.

"Give me the short version first. If it still holds up, we'll expand it."
